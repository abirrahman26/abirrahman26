## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏏 [BPL DataVault — A BPL DBMS](https://github.com/abirrahman26)
> `Oracle APEX` · `HTML` · `CSS` · `SQL` · `PL/SQL`

A full-scale database management system for BPL 2024/25. Real-time CricSheet datasets (JSON → CSV) integrated into a normalized schema (1NF → BCNF). Features **51 interactive pages**, role-based access, 34 reports, 13 aggregate queries, and 9 charts for deep player, team & match analytics.

`Aug 2025 – Sep 2025` · *Team: Sudeepta Mandal, Md Julfiker Hossain, Md. Rashid Uz Zaman*

---

### 🗺️ [ByteTrip — Inter-District Travel Route Finder](https://github.com/abirrahman26)
> `Python` · `Flask` · `HTML` · `CSS`

A web-based route planner for Bangladesh that finds the shortest, cheapest, and fastest inter-district routes — factoring in real-world fare policies and transport constraints, not just GPS distance.

`Aug 2025 – Sep 2025`

---
